How to play

The duel

Two survivors, two towers, one winner. Players take turns. On your turn pick one of the 3 weapons drawn from your deck and throw it across the arena. Knock your rival's health to zero — or knock them into the pit — to win.

Crates break, red barrels and TNT explode and chain together. Head hits deal 1.5× damage.

Mouse / touch

  • Click a weapon card to choose it
  • Press & drag anywhere to pull back the slingshot
  • Release to throw — drag further for more power
  • Release close to the start point to cancel

Keyboard

  • ←→ / AD aim angle
  • ↑↓ / WS throw power
  • Space / Enter throw
  • 123 or QE pick weapon
  • Esc / P pause · M mute · F full screen
  • Menus: ↑↓←→ move, Enter select, Esc back

Progress

Every win pays coins and unlocks the next arena. Spend coins on new Heroes and stronger weapons — Common, Rare, Epic and Mythic — then build your perfect 8-card deck in the Arsenal.
